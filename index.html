2. Picture the exemplar response

What would a successful student say or write?

The prototype creates a draft from the objective, student task, and any lesson content you provide. For the most specific exemplar, paste the key facts, text, data, source information, or worked example students should use. The exemplar will intentionally model varied language across the response, inside sentences, and at the word/phrase level. For older students, choose sustained discourse so the model reflects expectations for extended, connected language.
Planning principle: The app looks for language in the response students are expected to produce. It does not decide what must be taught.
